This survey is part of a research study conducted by Dr Varun Dutt at the Indian Institute of Technology Mandi, Himachal Pradesh, India - 175075.

The purpose of the survey is to find out how moral retention can be practiced through a number of interventions.

PROCEDURES

During the study, you will first be asked to provide a basic demographic profile of yourself, following which you will be presented with a self-reported questionnaire. You have to attempt all the questions presented in the survey.

PARTICIPANTS REQUIREMENTS

Participation in this study is limited to individuals aged 18 and above, with at least basic computer proficiency and the ability to read and understand English.

RISKS

The risks and discomfort associated with participation in this study are no greater than those ordinarily encountered in daily life or during other online activities.

BENEFITS

There may be no personal benefit from your participation in the study. However, the knowledge received may be of value to society.

COMPENSATION & COSTS

You will be compensated for completing the survey at the rate initially advertised. You will only be eligible for compensation if you have completed the survey in full and supplied the appropriate confirmation code. There is no partial payment if you do not complete the study. You will not be penalized if you choose to withdraw from the study without completing it, but you will not be compensated either.
